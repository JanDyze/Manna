# Manna logo

Traced from the chosen logo image. The symbol shapes were fitted to it and the lettering was rebuilt as clean, even strokes with rounded ends. Colours were measured from the image.

## Meaning

**Provision from above, received with open hands.**

- **The navy star is the gift coming down.** Manna came from heaven, fresh every morning. The four-pointed star is that light breaking through: *"Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above, and cometh down from the Father of lights"* (James 1:17).
- **The gold curve is the hand or vessel that receives it.** It's also the grain, the daily bread itself. It curves up and toward the star, the posture of someone gathering what was given, with thanks.
- **The gap between them matters.** The two shapes nearly meet but never merge: God provides, and we receive and steward. The gift is never ours by our own making.
- **Navy and gold:** navy for faithfulness and trust, the night sky manna fell from. Gold for provision, harvest and morning light. They are the same family of colours as the mascot's cape and clasp.
- **The rounded lettering** keeps it warm and approachable: a companion for everyday money decisions, not a bank.

The "dark" versions turn the navy parts cream for use on navy or dark backgrounds; the gold stays.
